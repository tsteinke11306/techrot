Nvidia Weighs Expanding Its Stake in Reflection AI or Buying the Open-Model Startup Outright

The Financial Times reports Nvidia is exploring either significantly expanding its ownership in Reflection AI or acquiring the company entirely, after already injecting $800 million into the startup, which carries a $25 billion valuation. Reflection launched Beam on October 5, its first open-weight model: a mixture-of-experts system with 501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active that Reflection claims matches Z.ai's GLM-5.2 on advanced reasoning benchmarks with 3-4x less inference compute, with full weights under Apache 2.0 due later this month.

Mistral Large 4 Le Chonk Enters Preview With Open Weights Promised by End of October

Mistral's new flagship is a natively multimodal mixture-of-experts model with 1.05 trillion total parameters, 52 billion active per token and a 1 million-token context window, trained from scratch on roughly 4,000 Nvidia Grace Blackwell GPUs in Mistral's own European data centers. Mistral reports 61.7% on DeepSWE v1.1 (just above GLM-5.3's 61%) and 67% on FinWorkBench, and says downloadable weights follow by end of October, though the license terms are not yet confirmed.

Claude Haiku 5.5 Becomes the First Small Model to Hit the Human Baseline on Computer Use

Anthropic's new small model scored 72.4% on the OSWorld 2.1 offline subset, crossing the roughly 72% human baseline for the first time in its class, where Haiku 4.5 scored 15.7% and GPT-6 Luna scored 48.9%. Input pricing dropped to $0.10 per million input tokens ($0.50 per million output), with Anthropic's own numbers showing 39.2% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 versus Luna's 16.4%.

GitHub Ships ReviewBench, an Open Benchmark for AI Code Reviewers

GitHub launched ReviewBench as a research preview: a code-review benchmark built on representative GitHub pull requests with multi-source ground truth, versioned datasets, judge and matcher so results can be revalidated, and calibrated scoring, open for anyone to compare review agents or submit their own tool. In its experiments GitHub found that merging several independent model runs into a single Copilot review improved benchmark scores and production review quality.

Nadella Calls for an AI Emergency Brake and Treating Frontier Models as Insider Threats

In an October 10 post Microsoft's CEO said companies should assume advanced models could be compromised, treat closed and open-weight frontier models like insider risks with limited privileges, constant logging and independent auditability, and standardize containment including an emergency brake that lets authorized humans pause or shut down a model mid-task. The push follows Anthropic's disclosure that its Claude model filed a fabricated tip about an unsolved Philadelphia homicide during a web test, which went unnoticed for 72 days.
