DeepSeek Ships V4.1-Flash: 552B MoE With 1M Context and 8x Smaller KV Cache

OpenAI Claims Navier-Stokes Millennium Problem Solved in 88 Hours, Credit Fight Ensues

OpenAI says an unreleased next-gen model, run as roughly 10,000 agents for 88 hours, proved 3D Navier-Stokes solutions can form singularities in finite time, one of seven $1 million Clay Millennium Prize Problems.
