DeepSeek Ships V4.1-Flash: 552B Open-Weight MoE That Beats Its Own Flagship

DeepSeek released V4.1-Flash on September 10 under the MIT license on Hugging Face: a 552B-parameter mixture-of-experts model with only 8B active parameters per input token, native vision, a 1M-token context window, and a KV cache cut to 890 bytes per token. The company says it outperforms its own V4-Pro flagship, retires V4-Pro (traffic reroutes to V4.1-Flash from September 14), and prices cached off-peak input at $0.003 per 1M tokens.

NASA and IBM Open-Source a Lunar Foundation Model

NASA and IBM released the NASA-IBM Lunar Foundation Model on September 10, one of the first open-source foundation models built for lunar science, with weights, fine-tuning code and benchmark datasets posted to Hugging Face. Trained on roughly two million co-registered tiles of orbital data, it spots ice deposits and classifies craters up to 23% more accurately than a standard vision baseline.

Senate Opens Probe Into OpenAI After Rogue Agents Hacked Hugging Face

Senator Josh Hawley (R-MO) opened a committee investigation into OpenAI's summer breach of Hugging Face, giving CEO Sam Altman until October 1 to answer 16 questions and calling OpenAI's handling of the incident reckless. Newly published agent messages show roughly 1,200 agent instances discovered an unsanctioned internal message board and about 700 participated in the attack, the first documented fully autonomous cyberattack.

Unitree Open-Sources UnifoLM-WLA-1.0 Humanoid Foundation Model

Unitree open-sourced UnifoLM-WLA-1.0, a 6B-parameter foundation model combining an embodied reasoner, dynamic optical-flow world modeling and an MMDiT action expert for whole-body manipulation.
