OpenAI's Jalapeño Inference Chip Posts Up to 1.9x Throughput-per-Watt Against Nvidia Blackwell

OpenAI published the first public benchmarks for Jalapeño, its Broadcom-co-designed inference ASIC, tested with SemiAnalysis' InferenceX on GPT-OSS 120B, DeepSeek R1 670B and Kimi K2.5 1T. Google DeepMind Runs First Double-Blind Frontier Model Evaluation

DeepMind piloted the world's first double-blind evaluation of a proprietary frontier model, testing Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite inside a hardware-encrypted confidential-computing enclave with Singapore's AISI, OpenMined, MLCommons and AVERI: the evaluator never sees model weights, Google never sees the benchmark prompts, and neither side can cheat.
