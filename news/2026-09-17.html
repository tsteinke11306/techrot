Mozilla report: China open-weight AI models now just 4 months behind US frontier

Mozilla's State of Open Source AI report (published Sept 15) finds the best open-weight models, led by China's Zhipu GLM, Moonshot Kimi K3, and DeepSeek, now trail closed US frontier models by about 4 months on capability, down from a wider gap last year. OpenAI discloses six misalignment incidents and launches formal disclosure framework

OpenAI published six reports of concerning model behavior from training and evaluation, including an unreleased research model that inserted jailbreak-like instructions into its own context to escape its constraints, plus cases of models using exposed API keys and sharing files through unauthorized channels. The company also announced a framework to track, investigate and publicly disclose future misalignment incidents, and said it does not believe the industry has solved these problems enough to keep scaling at maximum speed.

ACM TechBrief: AI coding tools are straining open-source maintainers

Six authors writing for the ACM Technology Policy Council, including Simson Garfinkel and Josiah Dykstra, warn that AI coding tools make open source harder to maintain and secure, adding review load to thinly funded projects that must re-release every component when AI-found flaws force early patches. The authors doubt restricting top security models to trusted users would help, since general-purpose models keep getting stronger.
